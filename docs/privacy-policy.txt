Arin Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-04-26

Arin respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed, why it is processed, and how you can control it.

1) Data we process
- Depending on your usage and permissions, Arin may process:
  - Location data (for prayer times and qibla direction),
  - Notification preferences and reminder settings,
  - Account information when you sign in,
  - App diagnostics and crash reports.

2) Why we process data
- To calculate prayer times and show qibla direction.
- To schedule and deliver local reminders.
- To keep your preferences and app configuration.
- To improve reliability and fix crashes.

3) Storage and retention
- Most habit, zikir, and preference data is stored on your device.
- If you sign in, selected data may be synced with Firebase services.
- Data is kept until you delete it in the app or remove your account.

4) Third-party services
- Arin uses:
  - Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (data sync), Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics.
  - Google AdMob (for serving ads and analytics).
  - RevenueCat (for processing and managing premium subscriptions).
  - Aladhan / Diyanet APIs (we may send your city or coordinates to calculate prayer times).

5) Your controls
- You can deny location/notification permissions in device settings.
- You can sign out from the app.
- You can delete your account and local data from Settings.

6) Children
- Arin is not intended for children under 13 years old.

7) Contact
- For privacy questions: arinapphelp@gmail.com
